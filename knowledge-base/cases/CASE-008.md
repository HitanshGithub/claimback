- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint No. MUM-H-049-2021-1622 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2020-2021)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-26
- **Category:** non_payable_consumables (Non-payable items / consumables)

- **Policy:** New India Mediclaim Policy (sum insured Rs 2,00,000 + Rs 50,000 cumulative bonus)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 1,07,508
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 24,804
- **Outcome:** Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld)
- **Amount awarded:** not stated

## Facts
The complainant was admitted to a Mumbai hospital in December 2020 with a diabetic wound of the right leg and underwent split-skin grafting of the right foot with vacuum-assisted closure (VAC). Against a final bill of Rs 1,07,508, Rs 77,641 was settled cashless. He disputed the deductions, in particular Rs 7,392 for a drainage kit, which he said was not a dressing item.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Rs 2,700 of nursing/RMO charges are included in room charges; Rs 23,837 was deducted as non-medical expenses, including Rs 7,392 for the drainage kit, which is not payable under clause 4.4.17 and Annexure II (item 180 - 'any kit not specified').

## Decision
Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld). No specific amount quantified in the decision (or no amount awarded).

## Reasoning
The Forum held that the drainage kit is a non-medical item listed as not payable in Annexure II (item 180) of the policy, so the settlement was in order and no relief could be granted.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Clause 4.4.17 - non-medical items excluded
- Annexure II item 180 - any kit not specified

## Key quote
> "Drainage Kit which is not payable as per Annexure II (Item No.180)"
