## Facts
The complainant, insured for three consecutive years, developed sudden pain in the left eye in August 2020 and was diagnosed with central retinal vein occlusion. He was admitted to an Amritsar eye hospital on 25 August 2020 and discharged on 26 August 2020 after an intravitreal Accentrix injection, a stay of more than 24 hours. The claim of Rs 32,750 was rejected.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
No treatment was given on the day of admission, 24-hour hospitalisation was not needed and the patient could have been treated on OPD basis; CRVO with Accentrix injection is not in the day-care list, and the TPA treats such anti-VEGF injections as OPD procedures.

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). No specific amount quantified in the decision (or no amount awarded).

## Reasoning
The Ombudsman noted that the injection is given in an operation theatre under strict sterile conditions and needs post-procedure observation, and that because of technological advances most eye procedures no longer need long hospitalisation, with even minor eye incisions covered as day care. Treating the injection as eye surgery, the rejection was held improper and the admissible claim was ordered to be paid.

> "in my view the case is covered under eye surgery and hence payable"
