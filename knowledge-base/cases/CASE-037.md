# CASE-037: Claim documents - Allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. - **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-051-2021-1957 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2021-2022)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-23
- **Category:** documents (Claim documents)
- **Insurer type:** public sector general insurer - United India Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Arogya Raksha Policy (sum insured Rs 7,00,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 37,800
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 37,800
- **Outcome:** Allowed (policyholder won)
- **Amount awarded:** Rs 25,000

## Facts
The complainant had left-eye cataract surgery at a Mumbai hospital in February 2020 and claimed Rs 37,800. He complied with the TPA's requirements, but the claim stayed unsettled for more than a year because the insurer insisted on a separate invoice for the intraocular lens (Rs 7,300), which the hospital had not issued.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Claim kept pending for non-submission of the IOL invoice; in any case the policy limits cataract surgery to Rs 25,000.

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). Amount awarded: Rs 25,000.

## Reasoning
The hospital's final bill already showed the IOL charge along with surgeon, OT, anaesthesia and other heads, so insisting on a separate invoice and withholding the claim for over a year was unjustified. The claim was payable up to the policy's Rs 25,000 cataract limit, with interest at 2% above bank rate from one month after submission of the final documents.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Policy cap of Rs 25,000 for cataract surgery
- Interest for delayed settlement (2% above bank rate)

## Key quote
> "withholding the claim for over a year for such a small and reasonable amount is not justified"
