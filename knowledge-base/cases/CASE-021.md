# CASE-021: Pre-existing disease / non-disclosure - Dismissed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-044-2021-1586 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2020-2021)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-29
- **Category:** ped_non_disclosure (Pre-existing disease / non-disclosure)
- **Insurer type:** standalone health insurer - Star Health and Allied Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Star Comprehensive Insurance Policy (sum insured Rs 5,00,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 1,15,284
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 1,15,284
- **Outcome:** Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld)
- **Amount awarded:** not stated

## Facts
The complainant took the policy from 30 September 2019 and renewed it. In December 2020, in the second policy year, he was admitted to ICU for cortical venous thrombosis. Cashless and reimbursement of Rs 1,15,284 were refused and he was deleted from cover because he had been hospitalised in August 2018 for deep vein thrombosis of the right leg, which was not disclosed in the proposal. He said he had recovered fully with fitness certificates, the present illness was different, and he had disclosed everything to the agent.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Non-disclosure of DVT diagnosed and treated about a year before inception; the proposal answered 'no' to other diseases; claim repudiated under the non-disclosure condition and cover cancelled under Condition 12.

## Decision
Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld). No specific amount quantified in the decision (or no amount awarded).

## Reasoning
The Forum relied on the 2018 DVT discharge summary and IRDAI's definition of a pre-existing disease as one diagnosed or treated within 48 months before the policy. The ailment fell within that window and the complainant produced no evidence of disclosure to the agent, so the repudiation was held to be in accordance with the policy terms and no relief was granted.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- IRDAI definition of Pre-Existing Disease (48 months before policy)
- Star Comprehensive policy conditions 9 and 12 - non-disclosure and cancellation

## Key quote
> "the ailment suffered by the insured falls within 48 months prior to the effective date of the policy"
