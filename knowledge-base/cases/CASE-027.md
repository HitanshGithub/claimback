# CASE-027: Waiting period - Dismissed (Insurance Ombudsman, Pune, 2021)

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Pune
- **Case reference:** Complaint No. PUN-H-044-1920-0089 (Award No. IO/PUN/A/HI/ /2021-22)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-19
- **Category:** waiting_period (Waiting period)
- **Insurer type:** standalone health insurer - Star Health and Allied Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Family Health Optima Insurance Plan (sum insured Rs 3,00,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 77,330
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 77,330
- **Outcome:** Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld)
- **Amount awarded:** not stated

## Facts
The complainant's wife was covered from February 2017. About 19 months after inception, in September 2018, she was admitted to a Mumbai public hospital and underwent arthroscopic repair of a right supraspinatus (rotator cuff) tear after six months of shoulder pain. The claim (Rs 77,330) was rejected under the 24-month waiting period.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
24-month waiting period for all treatment of diseases of tendons, ligaments, fascia, bones and joints other than those caused by accident (waiting period 3(ii)(d)); the medical records showed no history of trauma.

## Decision
Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld). No specific amount quantified in the decision (or no amount awarded).

## Reasoning
The Forum found no record of any injury or fall - the pre-operative notes showed six months of pain - and noted that rotator cuff changes are commonly degenerative. Since an accidental cause was not established, the 24-month waiting period applied to a policy that had run only about 19 months, and the rejection was upheld.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Waiting period 3(ii)(d) - 24 months for diseases of tendon, ligament, fascia, bones and joints (other than by accident)

## Key quote
> "In view of the fact of accidental injury not getting established, the ailment attracts 24 months waiting period."
