# CASE-031: Day care treatment - Allowed (District Commission Ernakulam, 2023)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** District Commission Ernakulam (Ernakulam District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, Kerala)
- **Case reference:** CC/21/192 - Johny Milton v. Universal Sompo General Insurance Company Ltd. (as reported by LiveLaw)
- **Decision date:** 2023
- **Category:** day_care (Day care treatment)
- **Insurer type:** private general insurer - Universal Sompo General Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Health insurance policy covering the complainant's mother (plan not stated in report)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 27,720
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 27,720
- **Outcome:** Allowed (policyholder won)
- **Amount awarded:** Rs 27,720

## Facts
The complainant's mother, covered under his health policy, was diagnosed with myopic choroidal neovascular membrane in the left eye and received an intravitreal injection as an inpatient for day-care treatment, being discharged the same day. The reimbursement claim of Rs 27,720 was rejected. According to the report, a similar procedure undergone later that year was allowed by the insurer without objection.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Treatment with the Accentrix eye injection is an outpatient procedure not requiring hospitalisation.

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). Amount awarded: Rs 27,720.

## Reasoning
The Commission held that the procedure should be viewed as day-care treatment - one that would otherwise need more than 24 hours' hospitalisation but is done in less time because of technological advancement - and that IRDAI guidelines say intravitreal injections should not be excluded. It treated the insurer's inconsistent stand on a similar later claim as an unfair trade practice and ordered reimbursement plus Rs 20,000 compensation and Rs 10,000 litigation costs.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- IRDAI guidelines - intravitreal injections not to be excluded (modern treatment)
- Definition of day care treatment

## Key quote
> "This inconsistency in the Opposite Party's actions suggests an unfair trade practice"
