# CASE-007: Non-payable items / consumables - Allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). - **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-049-2021-1677 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2021-2022)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-29
- **Category:** non_payable_consumables (Non-payable items / consumables)
- **Insurer type:** public sector general insurer - The New India Assurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** New India New Mediclaim 2012 Policy (sum insured Rs 10,00,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 2,85,691
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 10,890
- **Outcome:** Allowed (policyholder won)
- **Amount awarded:** Rs 10,890

## Facts
The complainant underwent arthroscopic ACL reconstruction of the left knee at a Mumbai sports-medicine hospital in November-December 2019 and claimed Rs 2,85,691. The TPA initially deducted Rs 65,332 and later paid part of it after clarifications from the hospital. The remaining dispute was Rs 10,890 billed separately by the hospital for anaesthesia machine and gases; the treating doctor certified that these charges are patient-specific and not included in OT charges.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Anaesthesia machine and gases are consumables forming part of OT charges under the standard definition of OT and should not be billed separately; OT and anaesthesia charges in the bill had already been paid. (Separately, Rs 17,947 of items such as caps, gauze, masks, gloves and food were deducted as non-payable.)

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). Amount awarded: Rs 10,890.

## Reasoning
The Forum noted that the hospital had billed the anaesthesia equipment and gases separately and clarified they were not part of OT charges; had they been included in OT charges the insurer would have paid them. Disallowing them merely because of how the hospital structured its bill was not justified, so the further Rs 10,890 was ordered to be paid.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Standard definition of operation theatre (OT) charges
- IRDAI list of non-payable items (items such as caps, masks, gloves, gauze)

## Key quote
> "since the hospital has not included these charges in the O.T. charges and has billed for it separately, disallowance of the same is not justified and cannot be sustained"
