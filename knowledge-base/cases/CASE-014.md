# CASE-014: Sub-limit / capping - Allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-051-2021-1789 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2020-2021)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-28
- **Category:** sub_limit (Sub-limit / capping)
- **Insurer type:** public sector general insurer - United India Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Individual Health Insurance Policy - Gold (sum insured Rs 5,50,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 1,44,338
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 1,04,338
- **Outcome:** Allowed (policyholder won)
- **Amount awarded:** Rs 97,500

## Facts
The complainant had right-eye cataract surgery at a Mumbai eye institute in January 2020 under a policy running from 18 April 2019. The claim of Rs 1,44,338 was settled for Rs 40,000, with surgeon, procedure and implant charges deducted as higher than reasonable and customary. The complainant relied on the policy condition limiting cataract claims to actual expenses or 25% of the sum insured, whichever is less.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Charges exceeded reasonable and customary rates (clause 3.33); the insurer also said the Gold policy capped cataract surgery at Rs 40,000 per eye under revised terms introduced from 25.03.2019, with all renewals after 25.06.2019 issued on the new terms.

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). Amount awarded: Rs 97,500.

## Reasoning
The Forum found that the complainant's policy was issued before the revised terms became compulsory and he had not opted for them, so condition 1.2.1 (actual expenses or 25% of sum insured, whichever is less) applied. Where the policy itself fixes the cataract limit, deducting under the reasonable & customary clause was not justified; the entitlement was Rs 1,37,500 (25% of Rs 5,50,000), so a further Rs 97,500 was payable.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Condition 1.2.1 - cataract limit: actual expenses or 25% of sum insured, whichever is less
- Clause 3.33 - reasonable and customary charges
- Revised Gold policy terms with Rs 40,000 per-eye cataract cap (effective 25.03.2019 / compulsory for renewals after 25.06.2019)

## Key quote
> "the deduction of claim amount on the ground of Reasonable and Customary Clause of the policy is not justified, the claim has to be paid as per policy terms and conditions"
