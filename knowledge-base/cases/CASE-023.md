# CASE-023: Waiting period - Allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-049-2021-1711 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2020-2021)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-25
- **Category:** waiting_period (Waiting period)
- **Insurer type:** public sector general insurer - The New India Assurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** New India Floater Mediclaim Policy (sum insured Rs 3,00,000, enhanced from Rs 1,00,000 in 2018-19)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 95,445
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 58,015
- **Outcome:** Allowed (policyholder won)
- **Amount awarded:** not stated

## Facts
The complainant, aged 64 and insured with the insurer since 2003, had enhanced his sum insured from Rs 1 lakh to Rs 3 lakh in 2018-19. In March 2020 he was hospitalised in Mumbai for abdominal wall cellulitis, myositis and right basal pneumonia, with newly detected uncontrolled diabetes. Against a bill of Rs 95,445 only Rs 30,289 was approved cashless, and reimbursement of the balance was denied.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Diabetes is a risk factor for cellulitis and diabetes/hypertension carry a 2-year waiting period that applies to the enhanced sum insured, so the claim was assessed on the earlier Rs 1 lakh sum insured, with room rent capped at 1% of that and proportionate deduction of other charges.

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). No specific amount quantified in the decision (or no amount awarded).

## Reasoning
The Forum held that diabetes is not the only cause of cellulitis, which can follow any break in the skin, and that the treatment also covered myositis and pneumonia unrelated to diabetes. Applying the enhanced-sum-insured waiting period for diabetes was therefore not justified, and the insurer was directed to recompute the claim on the full Rs 3,00,000 sum insured (less non-medicals) and pay the balance.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Two-year waiting period for diabetes and hypertension applied to enhanced sum insured
- Room rent 1% of sum insured with proportionate deduction

## Key quote
> "Diabetes is not the only risk factor for Cellulitis"
