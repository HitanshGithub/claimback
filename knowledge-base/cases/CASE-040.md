# CASE-040: Other exclusion or condition - Dismissed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-044-2021-1699 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2020-2021)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-30
- **Category:** other (Other exclusion or condition)
- **Insurer type:** standalone health insurer - Star Health and Allied Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Star Comprehensive Insurance Policy (sum insured Rs 7,50,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 3,84,774
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 3,84,774
- **Outcome:** Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld)
- **Amount awarded:** not stated

## Facts
The complainant, covered with the insurer since 2015, underwent laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy (bariatric surgery) for obesity with BMI 43, sleep apnoea and osteoarthritis at a non-network Pune hospital in September 2020. She had intimated the claim within 24 hours of admission, and her agent had been told by customer care that bariatric surgery was claimable up to Rs 2,50,000; no one told her it had to be done cashless at a network hospital. The reimbursement claim of Rs 3,84,774 was rejected.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Obesity is not covered under the main hospitalisation section; bariatric surgery is payable only under Section 6 as an additional benefit up to Rs 2,50,000, on a cashless basis with prior approval, subject to special conditions (36-month waiting period, BMI criteria, opinion of two surgeons).

## Decision
Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld). No specific amount quantified in the decision (or no amount awarded).

## Reasoning
The Forum held that it cannot go beyond the regulator-approved policy terms, under which bariatric surgery is payable only on a cashless basis with prior approval, so the reimbursement claim failed. It nevertheless recorded that the agent and insurer had not guided the policyholder about this condition and directed the insurer to prevent such lapses.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Star Comprehensive policy Section 6 - bariatric surgery benefit (cashless only, prior approval, special conditions)

## Key quote
> "the complainant was not provided timely guidance in respect of the policy conditions by the Agent and the Company officials as well"
