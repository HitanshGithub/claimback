# CASE-034: Modern treatment methods - Partly allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-049-2021-1939 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2021-2022)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-28
- **Category:** modern_treatment (Modern treatment methods)
- **Insurer type:** public sector general insurer - The New India Assurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** New India Mediclaim Policy (sum insured Rs 1,00,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 49,991
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 49,991
- **Outcome:** Partly allowed (policyholder partly won)
- **Amount awarded:** not stated

## Facts
The complainant's mother, insured for more than 20 years, was treated at an eye clinic for choroidal neovascular membrane of the left eye with three intravitreal anti-VEGF (Zaltrap) injections at six-week intervals between September and December 2020, each without overnight stay. Three claims totalling Rs 49,991 were rejected.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Hospitalisation was less than 24 hours and the procedure is not in the day-care list (clauses 2.16 and 2.10).

## Decision
Partly allowed (policyholder partly won). No specific amount quantified in the decision (or no amount awarded).

## Reasoning
The Forum accepted that intravitreal injection is an advancement of medical technology that does not need 24 hours' hospitalisation and cited IRDAI's Master Circular of 22.07.2020, which lists intravitreal injections among modern treatments that must not be excluded. However, noting divided medical opinion on whether it is an inpatient or outpatient procedure and the repetitive nature of the treatment, it directed that the cost be shared equally and awarded 50% of the admissible expenses.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Policy clauses 2.16 (hospitalisation) and 2.10 (day care)
- IRDAI Master Circular on Standardization of Health Insurance Products, 22.07.2020 - intravitreal injections

## Key quote
> "the cost of the treatment is to be shared equally between the complainant and the Respondent"
