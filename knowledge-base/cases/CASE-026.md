# CASE-026: Waiting period - Dismissed (Insurance Ombudsman, Chandigarh, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. - **Case reference:** Complaint Ref CHD-H-044-2021-0723
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-07
- **Category:** waiting_period (Waiting period)
- **Insurer type:** standalone health insurer - Star Health and Allied Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Medi Classic Insurance Policy (Individual), sum insured Rs 3,00,000
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 2,22,249
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 2,22,249
- **Outcome:** Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld)
- **Amount awarded:** not stated

## Facts
The complainant took a fresh individual policy starting 22 May 2020. She was admitted to a Ludhiana hospital on 24 June 2020 with fever and viral pneumonia, tested positive for Covid-19 and was discharged on 7 July 2020. Cashless was refused and the reimbursement claim (Rs 2,22,249 sought) was repudiated because the doctor's pre-authorisation form recorded shortness of breath for 4 days before admission, placing the onset within the first 30 days of the policy.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
30-day initial waiting period (Excl. 03): expenses for treatment of any illness within 30 days of first policy commencement are excluded (except accidents), and the insured was symptomatic within that period.

## Decision
Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld). No specific amount quantified in the decision (or no amount awarded).

## Reasoning
The Ombudsman held that the illness was contracted within the first 30 days of a fresh policy, as shown by the treating doctor's pre-authorisation form noting symptoms for four days before admission. The waiting-period exclusion therefore applied and there was no deficiency on the insurer's part.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Excl. 03 - 30-day initial waiting period (not applicable to accidents or after 12 months of continuous cover)

## Key quote
> "The admission is due to illness and same falls under the waiting period."
