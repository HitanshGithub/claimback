# CASE-012: Reasonable and customary charges - Allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Pune, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Pune
- **Case reference:** Complaint No. PUN-H-035-2021-0368 (Award No. IO/PUN/A/HI/ /2021-22)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-30
- **Category:** reasonable_customary (Reasonable and customary charges)
- **Insurer type:** standalone health insurer - Manipal Cigna Health Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** ProHealth Protect family floater (sum insured Rs 5,50,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 1,56,193
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 1,19,601
- **Outcome:** Allowed (policyholder won)
- **Amount awarded:** Rs 1,16,203

## Facts
The complainant's wife was hospitalised for Covid-19 from 8 to 13 October 2020. The hospital bill was Rs 1,56,193, but the reimbursement claim was settled for only Rs 36,592. The complainant pointed out that a relative treated for Covid in the same hospital at the same time was reimbursed by another insurer with only a co-payment deduction.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Covid-19 has no established protocol or standardised costs, so the claim was settled at the General Insurance Council's schedule of Covid treatment rates: Rs 59,550 deducted as in excess of GIC rates, plus Rs 3,098 for non-medical items (masks, caps, sheets) and Rs 300 miscellaneous charges.

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). Amount awarded: Rs 1,16,203.

## Reasoning
The Forum observed that the GI Council tariff was never circulated to policyholders or disclosed at claim intimation, had not been reviewed as expected and did not match actual hospital charges. As per IRDAI guidelines Covid claims must be assessed under each policy's terms; this policy had no head-wise ceilings and the insurer raised no specific objection on the reasonableness of the bill. Only the non-payable items (Rs 3,098) and miscellaneous charges (Rs 300) were upheld and the balance Rs 1,16,203 was awarded.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- General Insurance Council schedule of rates for Covid-19 treatment (held not binding for reimbursement under this policy)
- IRDAI guidelines that Covid claims be assessed as per the respective policy terms

## Key quote
> "The tariff given by GI council is not circulated amongst the policy holders nor was the complainant made aware of it at the time of claim intimation."
