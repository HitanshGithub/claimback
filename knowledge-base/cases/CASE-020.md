# CASE-020: Pre-existing disease / non-disclosure - Partly allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Pune, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Pune
- **Case reference:** Complaint No. PUN-H-053-1920-0101 (Award No. IO/PUN/A/HI/ /2021-22)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-22
- **Category:** ped_non_disclosure (Pre-existing disease / non-disclosure)
- **Insurer type:** standalone health insurer - Cigna TTK Health Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Cigna TTK Pro Health Insurance (sum insured Rs 10 lakh), ported from another standalone health insurer
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 2,17,724
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 2,17,724
- **Outcome:** Partly allowed (policyholder partly won)
- **Amount awarded:** Rs 77,825

## Facts
The complainant and her husband had a health policy with another insurer from 2015 and ported to the respondent in 2017, declaring the husband's diabetes. In the fourth policy year the husband was hospitalised several times - including angioplasty and then treatment for acute lymphoblastic leukaemia - and died in August 2018. The hospitalisation claim of Rs 2,17,724 was rejected because hypertension since 2013 had not been disclosed. At the hearing the complainant accepted that hypertension pre-dated the first policy and said the agent had advised them not to declare it.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Non-disclosure of hypertension since 2013 (certified by a hospital) in breach of the Duty of Disclosure clause (General Terms VIII), with the admission said to be related to the pre-existing illness.

## Decision
Partly allowed (policyholder partly won). Amount awarded: Rs 77,825.

## Reasoning
Given that hypertension was admittedly undisclosed but the claim was for cancer treatment, the Forum asked the insurer to settle the claim excluding hypertension-related expenses. The insurer computed Rs 77,825, and the Forum awarded that amount, with interest at bank rate plus 2% if not yet paid.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- General Terms and Conditions VIII - Duty of Disclosure
- Portability of health insurance

## Key quote
> "RI was asked to consider the claim for CA by excluding HTN related expenses, it being as an undisclosed ailment."
