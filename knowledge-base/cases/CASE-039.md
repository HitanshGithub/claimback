- **Policy:** New India Floater Mediclaim Policy (2018-19)

- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 1,39,185
- **Outcome:** Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld)
- **Amount awarded:** not stated

## Facts
The complainant's 21-year-old daughter, covered under the family floater policy (family insured since 2002), was admitted for 12 days in April-May 2019 for a lumbar slipped disc with sciatica that worsened after gym exercise. Treatment consisted of acupuncture, physiotherapy, massage, TENS, ultrasound and posture correction. The claim of Rs 1,39,185 was repudiated; the family said a similar claim at the same hospital had been paid in 2012, which the insurer disputed.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Acupuncture and physiotherapy treatment is excluded under exclusion clause 4.4.18.

## Decision
Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld). No specific amount quantified in the decision (or no amount awarded).

## Reasoning
The Forum found that the hospital treatment was essentially acupuncture and physiotherapy with virtually no other medical management, both of which are specifically excluded by clause 4.4.18. The repudiation was therefore in accordance with the policy and no relief was granted.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Exclusion clause 4.4.18 - acupuncture and physiotherapy

## Key quote
> "Exclusion Clause 4.4.18 specifically excludes Acupuncture & Physiotherapy treatment"
