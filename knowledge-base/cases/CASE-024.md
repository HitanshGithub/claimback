# CASE-024: Waiting period - Allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Pune, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Pune
- **Case reference:** Complaint No. PUN-H-044-1920-0100 (Award No. IO/PUN/A/HI/ /2021-22)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-22
- **Category:** waiting_period (Waiting period)
- **Insurer type:** standalone health insurer - Star Health and Allied Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Family Health Optima Insurance Plan (sum insured Rs 5,00,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 1,25,283
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 1,25,283
- **Outcome:** Allowed (policyholder won)
- **Amount awarded:** Rs 1,01,476

## Facts
The complainant's wife was covered from 17 November 2016; the policy expiring on 16 November 2017 was renewed 118 days late, from 15 March 2018. In February 2019 she underwent L5-S1 laminectomy with fixation and bone grafting in Pune. The claim of Rs 1,25,283 was rejected as falling within the 24-month waiting period for prolapse of intervertebral disc.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Claim arose in the second year of cover; the 24-month waiting period under exclusion 3(ii)(a) for prolapse of intervertebral disc (other than by accident) applies, computed by excluding the renewal gap period.

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). Amount awarded: Rs 1,01,476.

## Reasoning
The policy allowed a 120-day grace period for renewal with continuity of waiting-period benefits. As the renewal was within that grace period, the 24-month waiting period ran from first inception and ended on 16 November 2018, before the February 2019 surgery; the insurer was wrong to subtract the gap. The claim was payable at the insurer's own assessed liability of Rs 1,01,476, with interest if paid late.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Exclusion 3(ii)(a) - 24-month waiting period for prolapse of intervertebral disc
- Renewal clause - 120-day grace period with continuity of benefits

## Key quote
> "The clause implies that there will be continuity cover if renewed within grace period."
