# CASE-017: Co-payment - Partly allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-050-2021-1767 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/GI/ /2021-22)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-30
- **Category:** co_payment (Co-payment)
- **Insurer type:** public sector general insurer - The Oriental Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Happy Family Floater Policy (sum insured Rs 5,00,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 1,60,952
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 89,309
- **Outcome:** Partly allowed (policyholder partly won)
- **Amount awarded:** Rs 48,203

## Facts
The complainant, insured for more than 20 years, was hospitalised in Mumbai for Covid-19 in August 2020. His claim of Rs 1,60,952 was settled for Rs 71,643, and the deductions of Rs 89,309 were not explained to him. At the Forum's direction the insurer produced a break-up - excess room rent, proportionate doctor fees, investigations, PPE kits, miscellaneous charges, pre/post-hospitalisation items and a 10% co-payment of Rs 11,809 - and on review found a further Rs 34,638 payable.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Deductions made on the basis of the government notification for Covid claims, proportionate deduction for a higher room, non-payable items and the 10% co-payment applicable under the policy.

## Decision
Partly allowed (policyholder partly won). Amount awarded: Rs 48,203.

## Reasoning
After analysing the break-up, the Forum allowed PPE kit charges of Rs 13,565 over and above the Rs 34,638 already conceded by the insurer and held all other deductions, including the 10% co-payment, to be in order. The insurer was directed to pay Rs 48,203 in full and final settlement, subject to availability of sum insured.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- 10% co-payment under the policy
- Proportionate deduction for higher room category
- Government notification on Covid treatment charges (relied on by insurer)

## Key quote
> "the Forum is of the view to allow PPE Kit Charges of Rs.13565/- deducted over and above Rs.34638/-"
