# CASE-032: Modern treatment methods - Allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-051-2021-1697 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2020-2021)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-19
- **Category:** modern_treatment (Modern treatment methods)
- **Insurer type:** public sector general insurer - United India Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Family Medicare Policy 2014 (sum insured Rs 10,00,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 1,19,079
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 1,19,079
- **Outcome:** Allowed (policyholder won)
- **Amount awarded:** not stated

## Facts
The complainant, covered since 1997, was diagnosed with Hodgkin lymphoma and prescribed oral chemotherapy tablets, taken without hospitalisation. Three earlier claims for the same treatment had been rejected and then allowed by the Ombudsman, but five further claims for October 2020 to March 2021, totalling Rs 1,19,079, were again rejected. He also asked that no co-payment be applied.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Oral chemotherapy was taken on OPD basis, which is outside the scope of the policy under clause 1.1.

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). No specific amount quantified in the decision (or no amount awarded).

## Reasoning
The Forum held that modern cancer treatment such as oral chemotherapy cannot be denied, particularly when it is not specifically excluded, and cited IRDAI's Master Circular on Standardization of Health Insurance Products (22.07.2020), which requires listed modern treatments including oral chemotherapy to be covered as in-patient, domiciliary or day-care treatment. Following its earlier award for the same patient, it directed settlement of the admissible amount less non-medicals as per policy terms (including on co-payment), and held that compensation for harassment is outside its powers.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Policy clause 1.1 - scope of cover
- IRDAI Master Circular on Standardization of Health Insurance Products, 22.07.2020 - modern treatment methods including oral chemotherapy
- Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 - no compensation for mental agony

## Key quote
> "modern cancer treatment methods like oral chemo cannot be denied"
