# CASE-018: Pre-existing disease / non-disclosure - Allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-044-2021-1637 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2021-2022)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-30
- **Category:** ped_non_disclosure (Pre-existing disease / non-disclosure)
- **Insurer type:** standalone health insurer - Star Health and Allied Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Star Comprehensive Insurance Policy - 2015 (sum insured Rs 5,00,000 floater), ported
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 43,061
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 43,061
- **Outcome:** Allowed (policyholder won)
- **Amount awarded:** not stated

## Facts
The complainant had been continuously covered under mediclaim policies with a public sector insurer since 2000 and ported to the respondent insurer in August 2019 after his bank-linked product was discontinued. In the first month of the new policy he was hospitalised in Mumbai for acute internuclear ophthalmoplegia. The claim of Rs 43,061 was repudiated and his cover deleted because past bypass surgery (2013), facio-brachial paralysis, diabetes and hypertension were not disclosed in the porting proposal. He said he had submitted past discharge summaries and old policies with the claim and had no intention to mislead.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Non-disclosure of material facts (ischaemic heart disease with CABG, paralysis, diabetes, hypertension) while porting; claim repudiated under Condition 9 and the insured's cover deleted under Condition 14.

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). No specific amount quantified in the decision (or no amount awarded).

## Reasoning
Although the complainant had failed to disclose his health history while porting, the Forum held that under portability rules the new insurer must itself verify medical and claim history from the previous insurer when previous policy details are furnished. Having accepted the proposal and premium without doing so, it could not use non-disclosure to wipe out long-standing continuous cover, particularly as it had not shown that the present ailment was related to the undisclosed conditions. The insurer was directed to reinstate the cover and pay the admissible claim less non-medical expenses.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Star Comprehensive policy Condition 9 (non-disclosure) and Condition 14 (cancellation)
- Portability norms - verification of previous insurer's records

## Key quote
> "it is equally the responsibility of the Insurer to verify the medical as well as claims history of the persons to be insured, from the previous insurer while accepting the proposal"
