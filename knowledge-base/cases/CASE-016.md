# CASE-016: Co-payment - Allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, 2017)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman (centre not named in the published 2017-18 mediclaim award compilation)
- **Case reference:** Case No. 18 in the Individual Mediclaim award compilation - complainant v. Oriental Insurance Co. Ltd. (award dated 01.11.2017)
- **Decision date:** 2017-11-01
- **Category:** co_payment (Co-payment)
- **Insurer type:** public sector general insurer - The Oriental Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Oriental mediclaim policy (sum insured Rs 1,00,000); patient also covered under an employer policy with United India (sum insured Rs 3,00,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 70,160
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 70,160
- **Outcome:** Allowed (policyholder won)
- **Amount awarded:** Rs 70,160

## Facts
The complainant's mother was insured under an Oriental Insurance mediclaim policy and also under a policy taken through the complainant's employer with United India Insurance. She was hospitalised in Bareilly in May 2016 for coronary artery disease with diabetes and hypertension. United India settled the claim after deducting Rs 70,160 as 30% co-payment, and the balance claimed from Oriental was rejected.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
The co-payment portion deducted by one insurer cannot be claimed from another insurer.

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). Amount awarded: Rs 70,160.

## Reasoning
The complainant showed that the Oriental policy had no co-payment clause and no condition barring recovery of a co-payment deducted under another insurer's policy; by the policy's own definition, co-payment is a cost-sharing percentage that does not reduce the sum insured. The Ombudsman held the deduction on account of co-payment unjustified and directed the insurer to pay the co-payment amount to the insured.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Policy clause 2.8 - definition of co-payment (does not reduce sum insured)
- Contribution between multiple health policies

## Key quote
> "It was observed that deduction on account of co payment is unjustified."
