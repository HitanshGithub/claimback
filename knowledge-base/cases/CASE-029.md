# CASE-029: 24-hour hospitalisation requirement - Dismissed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-051-2021-1748 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2020-2021)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-12
- **Category:** hospitalization_24h (24-hour hospitalisation requirement)
- **Insurer type:** public sector general insurer - United India Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** Family Medicare Policy 2014 (sum insured Rs 2,00,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 93,490
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 93,490
- **Outcome:** Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld)
- **Amount awarded:** not stated

## Facts
The complainant, covered since 2009, was diagnosed with tuberculosis (MDR TB per the insurer) and, owing to the Covid pandemic, was treated at home on his doctor's advice. His first claim since inception, for Rs 93,490 of medical expenses, was rejected.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Treatment was on OPD basis; clause 1.2.1 covers hospitalisation of at least 24 hours or specified day-care treatment, and procedures usually done on an outpatient basis are not payable.

## Decision
Dismissed (insurer's decision upheld). No specific amount quantified in the decision (or no amount awarded).

## Reasoning
The Forum found there was no hospitalisation at all, the expenses related to outpatient/home treatment, and TB treatment is not a listed day-care procedure. As OPD treatment is outside the policy's cover, the repudiation was upheld despite sympathy for the complainant.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Clause 1.2.1 - minimum 24-hour hospitalisation; outpatient procedures not payable

## Key quote
> "Since OPD treatment is not covered under the Policy, the Company's decision of repudiation of the claim is as per the terms and conditions of the policy."
