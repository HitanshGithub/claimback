# CASE-010: Reasonable and customary charges - Allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-048-2021-1877 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2021-2022)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-16
- **Category:** reasonable_customary (Reasonable and customary charges)
- **Insurer type:** public sector general insurer - National Insurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** National Mediclaim Policy (sum insured Rs 3,00,000 + Rs 1,40,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 52,935
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 18,935
- **Outcome:** Allowed (policyholder won)
- **Amount awarded:** Rs 18,935

## Facts
The complainant's wife had right-eye cataract surgery at a Mumbai eye clinic in November 2019. The claim of Rs 52,935 was settled for Rs 34,000, citing the 'customary and reasonable' clause. The policy had no specific cap for cataract surgery, and the complainant argued that bigger hospitals would have charged more.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Reasonable and customary charges (clause 3.29): comparable Mumbai hospitals charged Rs 18,000-30,000 for cataract surgery; package rates are negotiated with network (PPN) hospitals; for non-PPN reimbursement claims only the cost of a monofocal lens is payable; the insured should act as if uninsured when agreeing to rates.

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). Amount awarded: Rs 18,935.

## Reasoning
The Forum held that PPN rates are negotiated between the insurer/TPA and specific hospitals and cannot be used as the benchmark for a non-network hospital. The policy did not require treatment at a PPN hospital, had no cap on cataract surgery and did not restrict cover to a monofocal lens, and the expenses were not unreasonable. The balance of Rs 18,935 was ordered to be paid, less non-medical expenses if any.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Clause 3.29 - reasonable and customary charges
- Preferred Provider Network (PPN) package rates

## Key quote
> "it would not be proper to compare the rates charged by non-PPN hospitals with that of PPN hospitals"
