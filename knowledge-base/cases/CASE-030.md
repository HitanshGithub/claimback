# CASE-030: Day care treatment - Allowed (Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai, 2021)

> **Rules-era note:** decided before the IRDAI health insurance rules of 29 May 2024. The outcome may rest on rules that have since changed (for example the old 48-month pre-existing disease waiting period or the superseded 2020 standardization circular). Check the current rule in rules_regulatory.json before relying on it.

- **Forum:** Insurance Ombudsman, Mumbai
- **Case reference:** Complaint Ref MUM-H-049-2021-1855 (Award No. IO/MUM/A/HI/ /2020-2021)
- **Decision date:** 2021-04-20
- **Category:** day_care (Day care treatment)
- **Insurer type:** public sector general insurer - The New India Assurance Co. Ltd.
- **Policy:** New India Mediclaim Policy (sum insured Rs 8,00,000)
- **Amount claimed:** Rs 49,225
- **Amount in dispute:** Rs 49,225
- **Outcome:** Allowed (policyholder won)
- **Amount awarded:** Rs 49,225

## Facts
The complainant, a breast cancer patient insured for about 23 years, was admitted to a Mumbai hospital on a day-care basis in December 2019, first for an intravenous zoledronic acid injection and ten days later for removal of her chemotherapy port in the operation theatre under local anaesthesia. Both claims, totalling Rs 49,225, were rejected.

## Insurer's reason for deduction / rejection
Hospitalisation was less than 24 hours and neither procedure is listed in the policy's day-care list (clauses 1 and 2.16); treatment could be done on OPD basis.

## Decision
Allowed (policyholder won). Amount awarded: Rs 49,225.

## Reasoning
The Forum treated the injection-based cancer therapy as an advancement of medical technology and noted that IRDAI's guidelines on standardisation of exclusions bar exclusion of such modern treatments; though those applied to policies from 01.10.2020, the treatment was not specifically excluded in this policy. Port removal, certified by the oncologist as requiring an OT under strict asepsis, was a day-care and not an OPD procedure. Both claims were ordered to be settled for Rs 49,225 less non-medical expenses, if any.

## Rules, clauses and precedents cited
- Policy clauses 1 and 2.16 - hospitalisation and day-care list
- IRDAI Guidelines on Standardization of Exclusions in Health Insurance Contracts - modern treatment methods

## Key quote
> "it has to be done in O.T. under strict aseptic conditions and is not an OPD procedure, the same should also be settled as a Day Care Procedure"
