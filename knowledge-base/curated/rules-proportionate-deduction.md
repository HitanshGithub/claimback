# IRDAI / regulatory rules: proportionate deduction

## R-POLICY-MUST-STATE-SUBLIMITS-PROPORTIONATE-DEDUCTIONS

A health policy must clearly state any sub-limits, proportionate deductions and package-rate provisions, with a cross reference to the relevant policy section. (The 2024 framework has no separate rule restricting which items proportionate deduction may apply to; that restriction lived in the superseded 2020 standardisation circular, so rely on the policy wording.)

> The sub-limits, Proportionate Deductions and the existence of Package rates if any, with cross reference to the concerned policy section,

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Schedule C, para 3.1(ix) (Matters to be stated in a Health Insurance Policy: 'A health insurance policy shall clearly state'), page 190 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Insurer applies a proportionate deduction, sub-limit or package-rate cap that is not clearly stated (with section reference) in the policy document; use with R-NO-DEDUCTION-OUTSIDE-LISTED-EXCLUSIONS and then check what the policy wording itself says about proportionate deduction.
