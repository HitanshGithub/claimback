# IRDAI / regulatory rules: ped

## R-PED-DEFINITION-36M

A condition is a pre-existing disease (PED) only if it was diagnosed by a physician, or medical advice/treatment was recommended or received, within 36 months before the policy commencement date. Conditions first diagnosed after the policy started, or only more than 36 months before it, do not meet this definition.

> "Pre-existing disease (PED)" means any condition, ailment, injury or disease: a) that is/are diagnosed by a physician not more than 36 months prior to the date of commencement of the policy issued by the insurer; or b) for which medical advice or treatment was recommended by, or received from, a physician, not more than 36 months prior to the date of commencement of the policy.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024, Schedule III, clause 1.6 (definition of Pre-existing disease), page 36 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-insurance-products-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: Insurer rejects or deducts a claim calling the condition 'pre-existing' although the first diagnosis/advice/treatment was after policy commencement or more than 36 months before it. Check that the policy is on a product compliant with the 2024 Regulations (existing products had to be aligned by 30.09.2024).
Challenge strength: strong

## R-PED-WAITING-MAX-36M

The waiting period for disclosed pre-existing diseases cannot exceed 36 months of continuous coverage.

> Waiting period for pre-existing diseases disclosed by the persons to be insured, shall be maximum up to 36 months of continuous coverage under the Health Insurance policy.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024, Schedule III, clause 7 (Pre-existing diseases and specific waiting period), page 37 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-insurance-products-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: Claim for a disclosed PED is rejected after 36+ months of continuous cover (including ported/migrated credit), or the policy wording still applies a longer (e.g. 48-month) PED waiting period.
Challenge strength: strong
