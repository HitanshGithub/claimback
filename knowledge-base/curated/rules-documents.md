# IRDAI / regulatory rules: documents

## R-NO-REJECTION-FOR-WANT-OF-DOCUMENTS-OR-DELAYED-INTIMATION

A claim cannot be rejected or closed merely because documents are pending or because the claim was intimated late.

> No claim shall be rejected or closed for want of documents or for delayed intimation of claim.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, V(1)(ii) (At the time of claim); also Section 2, Chapter V, para 22, page 120 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Insurer/TPA closes or rejects the claim citing 'documents not submitted', 'query not replied' or 'intimation beyond 24/48 hours / 30 days'.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-INSURER-COLLECTS-DOCUMENTS-FROM-HOSPITAL

After a claim is intimated, the insurer/TPA must collect the required documents from the hospital; the policyholder is not required to submit them.

> Pursuant to intimation of the claim, Insurers and Third-Party Administrators (TPAs), shall collect the required documents from the Hospitals. Policyholder shall not be required to submit the documents.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, V(3)(iii)(3) (Settlement of health insurance Claims), page 121 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: TPA demands that the patient obtain and submit hospital records (indoor case papers, bills, discharge summary) and delays/denies the claim for their absence.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-DOCUMENTS-CALLED-AT-ONE-GO

Insurers must ask for all necessary documents specified in the policy at one go, not in piecemeal queries.

> Call for all necessary documents as specified in the policy document at one go and avoid calling such documents/information in a piece meal manner;

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Protection of Policyholders' Interests, Operations and Allied Matters of Insurers) Regulations, 2024, Regulation 23(4) (Treatment of Policyholders and Claimants), page 25 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-protection-of-policyholders-interests-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: Repeated, successive document queries from the insurer/TPA that delay settlement, or demands for documents not listed in the policy document.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-CLAIM-DOCUMENTS-LISTED-IN-POLICY

The specific documents needed for a claim, and the claim procedure, must be listed in the policy document and on the insurer's website.

> necessary specific documentation required to support the claim are listed in the policy document along with the procedures to be followed for settlement of claims, in addition to placing the information prominently on their website.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Protection of Policyholders' Interests, Operations and Allied Matters of Insurers) Regulations, 2024, Regulation 22(1)(a) (Settlement of Claims), page 25 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-protection-of-policyholders-interests-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: Insurer asks for a document that is not in the policy's list of claim documents, or rejects for its absence.
Challenge strength: moderate
