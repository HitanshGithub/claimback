# Non-payable items in health insurance: List IV - Items that are to be subsumed into costs of treatment

Where this list is binding: it is printed as Annexure-A in Arogya Sanjeevani policy wordings (verified in the Niva Bupa 2026-27 and Star Health 2025-26 wordings) and in many other indemnity policy wordings. It binds as a contract term of the claimant's policy.

History: the list was standardised by IRDAI's Master Circular on Standardization of Health Insurance Products (IRDAI/HLT/REG/CIR/193/07/2020, 2020-07-22), which is superseded on 2024-05-29 by the Master Circular on Health Insurance Business (IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024), Annexure-6 item 1. No 2024-2026 regulation or circular re-issues the list, so do not describe it as a current IRDAI rule - cite the policy wording.

How to treat a deduction of an item on this list: The policy wording says this is subsumed into cost of treatment (incl. diagnostics), so a separate-line deduction is fair. At a network hospital you can ask the hospital to absorb it (a request, not an enforceable right since the 2020 circular was superseded).

Items NOT on any list, e.g. prescribed medicines, implants, sutures, catheters, IV fluids, are not 'non-payable' under these lists; if an insurer deducts one it must point to an exclusion or limit written in the policy document (R-NO-DEDUCTION-OUTSIDE-LISTED-EXCLUSIONS) and cite the clause (R-REJECTION-MUST-CITE-SPECIFIC-CLAUSE).
