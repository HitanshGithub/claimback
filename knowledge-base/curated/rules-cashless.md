# IRDAI / regulatory rules: cashless

## R-CASHLESS-AUTH-1HR

The insurer must decide a cashless authorisation request immediately and within one hour of receiving it (systems were to be in place by 31 July 2024).

> Insurer shall decide on the request for cashless authorization immediately but not more than one hour of receipt of request.

Source: Master Circular on IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations 2024 - Health Insurance (Master Circular on Health Insurance Business), Ref IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024, Chapter I, para 15(b) (Approval for Cashless facility), page 9 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-health-insurance-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-07-31.
Use when: Cashless pre-authorisation left pending for hours, forcing the patient to pay a deposit or convert to reimbursement.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-DISCHARGE-AUTH-3HR

Final cashless authorisation at discharge must be granted within 3 hours of the hospital's request, and the patient must not be made to wait for discharge.

> Insurer shall grant final authorization within three hours of the receipt of discharge authorization request from the hospital. In no case, the policyholder shall be made to wait to be discharged from the Hospital.

Source: Master Circular on IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations 2024 - Health Insurance (Master Circular on Health Insurance Business), Ref IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024, Chapter I, para 16(a) (Final authorization for Discharge), page 9 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-health-insurance-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-05-29.
Use when: Patient held back at discharge while the insurer/TPA delays final bill approval.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-DISCHARGE-DELAY-COST-ON-INSURER

If final authorisation is delayed beyond 3 hours, any additional amount the hospital charges (e.g. extra room-day charges) must be borne by the insurer from its shareholders' funds, not deducted from the policyholder.

> If there is any delay beyond three hours, the additional amount if any charged by the hospital shall be borne by the insurer from shareholder's fund.

Source: Master Circular on IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations 2024 - Health Insurance (Master Circular on Health Insurance Business), Ref IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024, Chapter I, para 16(b) (Final authorization for Discharge), page 9 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-health-insurance-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-05-29.
Use when: Bill includes an extra day's room/nursing charges because discharge approval took more than 3 hours, and the insurer deducts or passes it to the patient.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-DEATH-DURING-TREATMENT

If the policyholder dies during treatment, the insurer must immediately process the claim and get the body released from the hospital immediately.

> In the event of the death of the policyholder during the treatment, the insurer shall: i. immediately process the request for claim settlement. ii. get the mortal remains (dead body) released from the hospital immediately

Source: Master Circular on IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations 2024 - Health Insurance (Master Circular on Health Insurance Business), Ref IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024, Chapter I, para 16(c) (Final authorization for Discharge), page 9 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-health-insurance-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-05-29.
Use when: Hospital withholds the body pending insurer approval of the final bill.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-GROUP-MEMBER-DETAILS-NO-DENIAL

For group health policies, the insurer must obtain member details from the master policyholder so cashless works from day one, and no claim can be denied because member details were not available.

> In order to provide cashless claim settlement from the date of commencement of cover, the Insurer shall obtain the details of members of the group from the master policyholder, at the earliest. No claim shall be denied for non-availability of details of members of the group.

Source: Master Circular on IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations 2024 - Health Insurance (Master Circular on Health Insurance Business), Ref IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024, Chapter II, A.II (General Principles - group policies), page 13 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-health-insurance-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-05-29.
Use when: Employer/group policy claim denied or cashless refused because the employee/dependant 'is not yet in the member list'.
Challenge strength: strong
