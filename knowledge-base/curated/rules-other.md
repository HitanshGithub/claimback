# IRDAI / regulatory rules: other

## R-RENEWAL-NOT-DENIED-FOR-CLAIMS

A health policy's renewal cannot be refused because claims were made in earlier years (except benefit policies that terminate on payout, such as critical illness).

> An insurer shall not deny the renewal of a health insurance policy on the ground that the insured had made a claim or claims in the preceding policy years, except for benefit based policies where the policy terminates following payment of the benefit covered under the policy like critical illness policy.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024, Schedule III, clause 9.2 (Renewal of health policies), page 38 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-insurance-products-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: Insurer refuses renewal (or threatens non-renewal) after the policyholder made or disputed a claim.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-AYUSH-PARITY-POLICY

Each insurer must have a Board-approved AYUSH coverage policy that addresses placing AYUSH treatment at par with other treatments so policyholders can choose their treatment.

> Insurers shall have a Board approved policy for providing AYUSH coverage, which interalia, shall include their approach towards placing AYUSH treatments at par with other treatments for the purpose of health insurance so as to provide an option for the policyholders to choose treatment of their choice.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024, Schedule III, clause 5 (AYUSH coverage), page 37 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-insurance-products-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: AYUSH (Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Homeopathy) hospitalisation claim is rejected or capped far below allopathic equivalents; check the policy wording's AYUSH clause and ask for the insurer's AYUSH policy. Coverage still depends on the specific policy.
Challenge strength: context_dependent

## R-MODERN-TREATMENTS-ENDEAVOUR

Insurers are to endeavour to cover modern/advanced treatments such as robotic surgery, oral chemotherapy, immunotherapy injections, intravitreal injections, stereotactic radiosurgery, deep brain stimulation, balloon sinuplasty, IONM and stem-cell therapy for haematological conditions. This is an 'endeavour' obligation; actual cover and sub-limits depend on the policy wording.

Effective: 2024-05-29.
Use when: Claim for a modern treatment (e.g. robotic surgery, oral chemo, immunotherapy, intravitreal injection) is rejected as 'not covered/experimental'; supports the argument together with the policy's own modern-treatment clause.

## R-NO-DEDUCTION-OUTSIDE-LISTED-EXCLUSIONS

All applicable exclusions must be written into the policy document; the insurer cannot make further deductions from a claim in the name of any exclusion not in the policy.

> Insurers shall mandatorily include in the policy document all the applicable exclusions for a particular product. No further deductions shall be made from the claim amount in the name of any other exclusions.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 2, Chapter IV, para 16.3 (General Principles governing issuance of policies), page 144 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Deductions labelled as 'non-payable', 'non-medical', 'not covered' or similar that cannot be traced to an exclusion or limit actually written in the policy document.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-CIS-POLICY-DOCUMENT-PREVAILS

If the Customer Information Sheet and the policy document conflict, the policy document's terms prevail.

> In case of any conflict, the terms and conditions mentioned in the policy document shall prevail.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Schedule D, Customer Information Sheet (Health), Note (ii), page 197 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Caution when a policyholder relies only on the CIS: always verify the limit/exclusion in the full policy wording before challenging.
Challenge strength: context_dependent

## R-MULTIPLE-POLICIES-PRIMARY-INSURER

With several indemnity health policies, the policyholder chooses which policy to claim under; that insurer becomes primary and must coordinate with the other insurers for any balance amount.

> a) If a policyholder has more than one health insurance policy from different insurers he/she can file for claim settlement as per his/her choice under any policy. b) The Insurer of that chosen policy shall be treated as the primary Insurer.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, V(5)(1) (Claims in respect of multiple Policies - Indemnity), page 122 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Insurer insists the claim be filed first under another policy (e.g. employer group cover) or refuses to coordinate settlement of the balance.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-FREE-LOOK-30-DAYS

New individual health policies of one year or more have a 30-day free-look period from receipt of the policy document to review and cancel.

> Every policyholder of life and new individual health insurance policies, except for those policies with tenure of less than a year, shall be provided a free look period of 30 days beginning from the date of receipt of policy document, whether received electronically or otherwise, to review the terms and conditions of such policy.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Protection of Policyholders' Interests, Operations and Allied Matters of Insurers) Regulations, 2024, Regulation 20(1) (Free Look Period of Life and Health Policies), page 24 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-protection-of-policyholders-interests-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: Policyholder discovers restrictive terms (sub-limits, room rent caps, co-pay) soon after buying and wants to cancel.
Challenge strength: moderate
