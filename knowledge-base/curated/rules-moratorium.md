# IRDAI / regulatory rules: moratorium

## R-MORATORIUM-60M

After 60 continuous months of health cover (portability and migration periods count), the insurer cannot contest the policy or any claim on grounds of non-disclosure or misrepresentation, except for established fraud.

> After completion of sixty continuous months of coverage (including portability and migration) in health insurance policy, no policy and claim shall be contestable by the insurer on grounds of non-disclosure, misrepresentation, except on grounds of established fraud. This period of sixty continuous months is called as moratorium period.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024, Schedule III, clause 8 (Moratorium), page 37 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-insurance-products-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: Insurer rejects a claim or cancels the policy for non-disclosure/misrepresentation (e.g. undisclosed PED) after 5+ years of continuous cover, without proving fraud. Applies to policies of general and health insurers.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-MORATORIUM-ENHANCED-SI

The 5-year moratorium runs on the sum insured of the first policy; for any later increase in sum insured, a fresh 5 years applies only to the enhanced portion.

> The moratorium would be applicable for the sums insured of the first policy and subsequently completion of Five continuous years would be applicable from date of enhancement of sums insured only on the enhanced limits.

Source: Annexures to Master Circular on Health Insurance Business dated 29.05.2024, Annexure-1 (Customer Information Sheet), row 12 'Things to remember' - Moratorium Period, page 5 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-health-insurance-2024-annexures.pdf). Effective: 2024-05-29.
Use when: Insurer uses non-disclosure to deny the whole claim after 5 years where only the sum insured was enhanced recently: the original sum insured remains protected by the moratorium; only the enhanced amount can still be contested.
Challenge strength: context_dependent

## R-MORATORIUM-PERMANENT-EXCLUSIONS-SURVIVE

Even after the moratorium, the policy is non-contestable except for proven fraud, but permanent exclusions written in the policy contract still apply.

> After the expiry of Moratorium Period no health insurance policy shall be contestable except for proven fraud and permanent exclusions specified in the policy contract.

Source: Annexures to Master Circular on Health Insurance Business dated 29.05.2024, Annexure-1 (Customer Information Sheet), row 12 'Things to remember' - Moratorium Period, page 5 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-health-insurance-2024-annexures.pdf). Effective: 2024-05-29.
Use when: Assessing whether the moratorium defeats a rejection: it defeats non-disclosure/misrepresentation grounds, but not a genuine permanent exclusion in the policy wording.
Challenge strength: context_dependent
