# IRDAI / regulatory rules: claim timeline

## R-REIMBURSEMENT-SETTLEMENT-15-DAYS

Reimbursement (non-cashless) health claims must be settled within 15 days from submission of the claim.

> Settlement of claims (other than cashless) shall be settled within fifteen days from submission of claim.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, V(3)(iii)(6); also Schedule A Citizens' Charter (Health), item 4, page 121 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Reimbursement claim pending beyond 15 days after submission; combine with R-CLAIM-DELAY-INTEREST for interest on delay.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-CLAIM-DELAY-INTEREST

If a claim is not settled within the specified timelines, the claimant is entitled to interest at bank rate + 2% from the date of claim intimation until payment, payable by the insurer on its own (suo motu).

> In case the claim is not settled within the specified timelines, then the claimant is entitled for interest at bank rate plus 2 percent from the date of receipt of intimation to till the date of payment. Such interest shall be suo-moto paid by the insurers.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, V(2)(ii) (Processing of claim), page 120 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Claim paid late (beyond the 15-day reimbursement TAT or other specified timeline) without interest; demand interest in the grievance.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-BANK-RATE-DEFINITION

'Bank Rate' for interest calculations is the RBI bank rate prevailing on 1 April of the financial year in which the claim fell due.

> "Bank Rate" means Bank rate fixed by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) which is prevalent as on 1st day of the financial year in which the claim has fallen due.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Protection of Policyholders' Interests, Operations and Allied Matters of Insurers) Regulations, 2024, Regulation 5(3) (Definitions), page 19 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-protection-of-policyholders-interests-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: Computing the delay interest (bank rate + 2%) owed on a late claim payment.
Challenge strength: moderate
