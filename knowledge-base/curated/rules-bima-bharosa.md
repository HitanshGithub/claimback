# IRDAI / regulatory rules: bima bharosa

## R-BIMA-BHAROSA-ONLINE-COMPLAINT

Policyholders/claimants can also register a complaint online on IRDAI's Bima Bharosa portal.

> The policyholder or the claimant also has the option to register the complaint on-line at IRDAI's Bima Bharosa by visiting https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in/.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, VI(1) (Lodging of complaint), page 123 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Insurer is not responding, or the policyholder wants the complaint recorded on IRDAI's system and tracked.
Challenge strength: moderate

## R-BIMA-BHAROSA-INTEGRATION-TRACKING

Insurers must integrate their grievance portals with Bima Bharosa, with real-time mirroring, so grievances can be registered and tracked online.

> Insurers shall integrate its grievance portal with the Bima Bharosa portal to facilitate the registering/ tracking of grievance on-line by the policyholders.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, VI(1) (Lodging of complaint), page 123 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Complaint lodged with the insurer does not show up/track on Bima Bharosa, or status is not updated.
Challenge strength: moderate
