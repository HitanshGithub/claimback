# IRDAI / regulatory rules: non disclosure

## R-MATERIAL-INFORMATION-ONLY-WHAT-PROPOSAL-ASKED

'Material information' is the important, essential and relevant information and documents explicitly sought by the insurer in the proposal form; disclosure duties apply to both insurer and prospect.

> "Material Information" for the purpose of these regulations shall mean all important, essential and relevant information and documents explicitly sought by insurer in the proposal form.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Protection of Policyholders' Interests, Operations and Allied Matters of Insurers) Regulations, 2024, Regulation 5(10), Explanation (i) (definition of Proposal form / Material Information), page 20 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-protection-of-policyholders-interests-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: Insurer alleges non-disclosure of something the proposal form never explicitly asked about (e.g. a minor condition, lifestyle detail or question not in the form).
Challenge strength: moderate

## R-PROPOSAL-FORM-COPY-WITHIN-15-DAYS

The insurer must give the insured a copy of the proposal form submitted, along with the policy document, free of charge within 15 days of accepting the proposal.

> It is the duty of the insurer to furnish to the insured, with no additional charges, either a soft copy or a hard copy of the proposal form submitted by the prospect, along with the policy document within 15 days of the acceptance of a proposal.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 2, Chapter III, para 12.4 (Processing of the proposal Form), page 142 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Non-disclosure rejection: request the proposal form copy to check what was actually asked/declared (and whether it was filled by an agent); non-supply of the copy strengthens the grievance.
Challenge strength: moderate
