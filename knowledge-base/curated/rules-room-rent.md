# IRDAI / regulatory rules: room rent

## R-ROOM-ICU-LIMITS-IN-CIS

The Customer Information Sheet must show financial limits of coverage - sub-limits, co-payment (including any 'Room / ICU charges beyond' limit) and deductibles - that the policyholder will have to bear.

> In case of a claim, this policy requires you to share the following costs: Expenses exceeding the following Sublimits

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Schedule D, Customer Information Sheet (Health), row 8 'Financial limits of coverage' (template row 'Room / ICU charges beyond ----'), page 194 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Room rent/ICU cap or resulting deduction applied although no such limit appears in the CIS/policy schedule; context-dependent because the policy document prevails over the CIS (see R-CIS-POLICY-DOCUMENT-PREVAILS).
Challenge strength: context_dependent
