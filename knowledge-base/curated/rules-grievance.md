# IRDAI / regulatory rules: grievance

## R-SENIOR-CITIZEN-CHANNEL

Every insurer must run a separate channel for health claims and grievances of senior citizens and publish its details on its website.

> All insurers shall establish a separate channel to address the health insurance related claims and grievances of senior citizens. The details of such channel shall be available in the website of the insurers.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024, Schedule III, clause 11 (Special provisions for senior citizens), page 38 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-insurance-products-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: Claimant is a senior citizen - direct the claim follow-up/grievance to the insurer's dedicated senior-citizen channel and cite this if none is available.
Challenge strength: moderate

## R-GRIEVANCE-ACK-IMMEDIATE-RESOLVE-14-DAYS

Insurers must acknowledge a complaint immediately and resolve it within 14 days, giving reasons with specific reference to policy terms if the complaint is not accepted.

> On receipt of a complaint, the complainant will have to be given an acknowledgement immediately. Insurers should provide resolution to the complaint within 14 days along with the reasons for not accepting the complaint with specific reference to the relevant terms and conditions of the policy.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, VI(2)(i) (Turnaround time for resolution of complaints); also Schedule A Citizens' Charter (Health), item 6, page 123 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Grievance to the insurer's GRO not acknowledged, not resolved within 14 days, or rejected without citing specific policy terms.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-GRIEVANCE-FILING-MODES

A policyholder or beneficiary can complain directly to the insurer and/or its distribution channel (including the TPA) - at a branch, by letter/email, on the insurer's website or via its call centre.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, VI(1)(i) (Lodging of complaint), page 123 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Starting escalation: first written complaint to the insurer's Grievance Redressal Officer (and TPA if involved).
Challenge strength: moderate

## R-NO-BAR-ON-COMPLAINING-TO-IRDAI

Insurers and intermediaries must not prohibit, bar or discourage a policyholder or claimant from complaining to IRDAI.

> No insurer or distribution channel shall prohibit, bar or discourage any policyholder or claimant from lodging any grievance to the Authority.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Protection of Policyholders' Interests, Operations and Allied Matters of Insurers) Regulations, 2024, Regulation 25(3) (Grievance Redressal Procedure), page 25 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-protection-of-policyholders-interests-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: Settlement offers or discharge vouchers conditioned on not complaining, or agents/TPA discouraging a complaint.
Challenge strength: moderate

## R-GRIEVANCE-REPLY-MUST-GIVE-OMBUDSMAN-DETAILS

The insurer's response to any grievance must include contact details of the concerned Insurance Ombudsman for escalation.

> The response letter of the Insurer in any grievance shall include the contact details of concerned insurance ombudsmen where his/her complaint can be escalated in case, the policyholder is not satisfied by the grievance redressal provided by the Insurer.

Source: Master Circular on IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations 2024 - Health Insurance (Master Circular on Health Insurance Business), Ref IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024, Chapter I, para 19 (Redressal of Grievances of the Policyholder), page 10 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-health-insurance-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-05-29.
Use when: Insurer's grievance reply omits ombudsman details; point this out and escalate.
Challenge strength: moderate

## R-GRIEVANCE-UNRESOLVED-COMMUNICATE-OPTIONS

If the insurer does not resolve the complaint, it must tell the policyholder the options, including the Insurance Ombudsman or Consumer Court, within 14 days from the original date of receipt of the complaint.

> If complaint is NOT resolved by the Insurer, communicate the details to the Policyholder of options including referring the complainant to Insurance Ombudsman / Consumer Court.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Schedule A, Citizens' Charter (Health), item 6 'Complaints', page 157 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Insurer neither resolves nor informs escalation options within 14 days of the complaint.
Challenge strength: moderate
