# IRDAI / regulatory rules: rejection letter

## R-REJECTION-MUST-CITE-SPECIFIC-CLAUSE

Whenever a claim is repudiated, rejected or partly disallowed, the insurer must give full details with reference to the specific terms and conditions of the policy document.

> In case, the claim is repudiated or rejected or disallowed partially, details shall be communicated to the claimant along with full details giving reference to the specific terms and conditions of the policy document.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, V(3)(iii)(5); see also Health Master Circular Chapter I para 17(b), page 121 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Rejection/deduction letter says only 'not payable', 'as per policy terms', 'non-medical expenses' or 'reasonable and customary' without quoting the specific clause relied upon.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-REPUDIATION-NEEDS-CRC-APPROVAL

No claim may be repudiated without approval of the insurer's Product Management Committee or its three-member Claims Review Committee (CRC).

> No claim shall be repudiated without the approval of Product Management Committee (PMC) or a three-member sub-group of PMC called the Claims Review Committee (CRC).

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, V(3)(iii)(4); see also Health Master Circular Chapter I para 17(a) and Chapter II A.I(4)(b), page 121 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Full rejection issued by a TPA or claims officer; ask the insurer to confirm CRC approval of the repudiation.
Challenge strength: moderate
