# IRDAI / regulatory rules: ombudsman

## R-OMBUDSMAN-AFTER-30-DAYS-OR-UNSATISFACTORY-DECISION

A policyholder may approach the Insurance Ombudsman if the complaint is not resolved within 30 days or if the insurer's decision is not acceptable.

> *(The policyholder may approach the Insurance Ombudsman if his / her complaint is not resolved within 30 days or if the decision of the company is not acceptable to the policyholder.)

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Schedule A, Citizens' Charter (Health), note to item 6 'Complaints', page 157 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Deciding when escalation to the Ombudsman is permitted after a grievance to the insurer.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-OMBUDSMAN-CLAIMS-UP-TO-50-LAKH

If not satisfied with the insurer's grievance resolution, unresolved or partially resolved complaints can be escalated to the Insurance Ombudsman of the relevant jurisdiction where the claim amount is up to Rs 50 lakh.

> In case the complainant is not satisfied with the resolution of grievance provided by the insurer, they can escalate the unresolved / partially resolved complaints to Insurance Ombudsman of concerned jurisdiction, in case the claim amount is up to Rs. 50 lakhs.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, VI(3)(i) (Filing of complaint before Insurance Ombudsman), page 123 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Checking Ombudsman eligibility for a rejected/short-settled claim; above Rs 50 lakh use consumer commission/civil remedies.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-OMBUDSMAN-NO-FEE-FILING-MODES

An Ombudsman complaint is free of any charge/fee and can be filed in person, online at cioins.co.in, or in writing by post/email.

> The policyholder has option to take up the matter before insurance ombudsman of competent jurisdiction without any charge / fee through any of the following options: a. In person; b. Online by visiting https://cioins.co.in/Complaint/Online; c. In writing through post or through email by giving complete details.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, VI(3)(ii) (Filing of complaint before Insurance Ombudsman), page 123 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Preparing the Ombudsman complaint; reassure there is no fee and online filing is available.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-OMBUDSMAN-GROUNDS-DELAY-REPUDIATION

The Ombudsman handles complaints about delay in claim settlement beyond regulatory timelines and about partial or total repudiation of claims by health insurers.

> (a) delay in settlement of claims, beyond the time specified in the regulations, framed under the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India Act, 1999; (b) any partial or total repudiation of claims by the life insurer, General insurer or the health insurer;

Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (as amended up to 09.11.2023; CIO consolidated copy), Rule 13(1)(a) and (b) (Duties and functions of Insurance Ombudsman), page 8 (knowledge-base/regulations/insurance-ombudsman-rules-2017-consolidated-2023.pdf). Effective: 2023-11-09.
Use when: Any health claim rejection, partial deduction (short settlement) or delayed settlement.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-OMBUDSMAN-GROUND-NONCOMPLIANCE-WITH-IRDAI-RULES

The Ombudsman can also take up non-observance of IRDAI regulations, circulars, guidelines or the policy terms insofar as they relate to claim delay, repudiation, premium, misrepresentation, policy construction, servicing or issuance issues.

> any other matter arising from non-observance of or non-adherence to the provisions of any regulations made by the Authority with regard to protection of policyholders interests or otherwise, or of any circular, guideline or instruction issued by the Authority, or of the terms and conditions of the policy contract, insofar as such matter relates to issues referred to in clauses (a) to (h).

Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (as amended up to 09.11.2023; CIO consolidated copy), Rule 13(1)(i) (substituted by G.S.R. 147(E) dated 02.03.2021), page 8 (knowledge-base/regulations/insurance-ombudsman-rules-2017-consolidated-2023.pdf). Effective: 2023-11-09.
Use when: Complaint is based on breach of an IRDAI rule (e.g. cashless/discharge TAT, documents rule, rejection letter not citing a clause).
Challenge strength: strong

## R-OMBUDSMAN-PRECONDITION-APPROACH-INSURER-FIRST

Before going to the Ombudsman, the complainant must have made a written/email/online representation to the insurer, and either it was rejected, no reply came within one month of the insurer receiving it, or the reply is unsatisfactory.

> No complaint to the Insurance Ombudsman shall lie unless (a) the complainant 1 [has made a representation in writing or through electronic mail or online through website of the insurer or insurance broker concerned] named in the complaint and-- (i) either the insurer 1 [or insurance broker, as the case may be,] had rejected the complaint; or (ii) the complainant had not received any reply within a period of one month after the insurer 1 [or insurance broker, as the case may be,] received his representation; or (iii) the complainant is not satisfied with the reply given to him by the insurer 1 [or insurance broker, as the case may be.]

Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (as amended up to 09.11.2023; CIO consolidated copy), Rule 14(3)(a) (as amended by G.S.R. 147(E) dated 02.03.2021), page 9 (knowledge-base/regulations/insurance-ombudsman-rules-2017-consolidated-2023.pdf). Effective: 2023-11-09.
Use when: Checking whether an Ombudsman complaint is maintainable - ensure a written grievance to the insurer exists (claim rejection letter alone may not be enough; send a representation/grievance first).
Challenge strength: strong

## R-OMBUDSMAN-ONE-YEAR-LIMIT

The Ombudsman complaint must be filed within one year of the insurer's rejection of the representation, of receiving its unsatisfactory decision, or of expiry of one month from sending the representation if no reply was received.

> (b) The complaint is made within one year-- (i) after the order of the insurer 1 [or insurance broker, as the case may be,] rejecting the representation is received; or (ii) after receipt of decision of the insurer 1 [or insurance broker, as the case may be], which is not to the satisfaction of the complainant; (iii) after expiry of a period of one month from the date of sending the written representation to the insurer 1 [or insurance broker, as the case may be,] if the insurer or 1 [ insurance broker, as the case may be], named fails to furnish reply to the complainant.

Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (as amended up to 09.11.2023; CIO consolidated copy), Rule 14(3)(b), page 9 (knowledge-base/regulations/insurance-ombudsman-rules-2017-consolidated-2023.pdf). Effective: 2023-11-09.
Use when: Calculating the deadline to approach the Ombudsman.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-OMBUDSMAN-DELAY-CONDONATION

The Ombudsman may condone delay in filing (beyond one year) after hearing the insurer's objections and recording reasons.

> The Ombudsman shall be empowered to condone the delay in such cases as he may consider necessary, after calling for objections of the insurer or insurance broker, as the case may be, against the proposed condonation and after recording reasons for condoning the delay

Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (as amended up to 09.11.2023; CIO consolidated copy), Rule 14(4), page 9 (knowledge-base/regulations/insurance-ombudsman-rules-2017-consolidated-2023.pdf). Effective: 2023-11-09.
Use when: The one-year limit has passed; request condonation with reasons for the delay.
Challenge strength: context_dependent

## R-OMBUDSMAN-BAR-IF-COURT-OR-FORUM-CASE

The Ombudsman cannot hear a complaint on the same subject matter that is pending before, or already decided by, a court, consumer forum or arbitrator.

> No complaint before the Insurance Ombudsman shall be maintainable on the same subject matter on which proceedings are pending before or disposed of by any court or consumer forum or arbitrator.

Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (as amended up to 09.11.2023; CIO consolidated copy), Rule 14(5), page 9 (knowledge-base/regulations/insurance-ombudsman-rules-2017-consolidated-2023.pdf). Effective: 2023-11-09.
Use when: Choosing between Ombudsman and consumer commission - do not file both on the same dispute.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-OMBUDSMAN-JURISDICTION

The complaint can be made by the aggrieved person or legal heirs/nominee/assignee to the Ombudsman covering either the insurer's branch/office complained against or the complainant's residence.

> Any person who has a grievance against an insurer 1 [or insurance broker] may himself or through his legal heirs, nominee or assignee, make a complaint in writing to the Insurance Ombudsman within whose territorial jurisdiction the branch or office of the insurer 1 [or the insurance broker, as the case may be,] complained against or the residential address or place of residence of the complainant is located.

Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (as amended up to 09.11.2023; CIO consolidated copy), Rule 14(1) (as amended by G.S.R. 147(E) dated 02.03.2021), page 9 (knowledge-base/regulations/insurance-ombudsman-rules-2017-consolidated-2023.pdf). Effective: 2023-11-09.
Use when: Selecting which Ombudsman office to file with; nominee/legal heir filing after death of the insured.
Challenge strength: moderate

## R-OMBUDSMAN-AWARD-CAP-50-LAKH

The Ombudsman cannot award more than the loss directly suffered, and the award cannot exceed Rs 50 lakh including expenses (raised from Rs 30 lakh on 9 Nov 2023).

> (i) not award any compensation in excess of the loss suffered by the complainant as a direct consequence of the cause of action; or (ii) not award compensation exceeding rupees 3 [fifty] lakhs (including relevant expenses, if any).

Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (as amended up to 09.11.2023; CIO consolidated copy), Rule 17(3), proviso (i)-(ii) ('thirty' substituted by 'fifty' vide Insurance Ombudsman (Amendment) Rules, 2023, G.S.R. 828(E) dated 09.11.2023), page 11 (knowledge-base/regulations/insurance-ombudsman-rules-2017-consolidated-2023.pdf). Effective: 2023-11-09.
Use when: Deciding whether the Ombudsman is the right forum for the disputed amount.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-OMBUDSMAN-AWARD-WITHIN-3-MONTHS

The Ombudsman should pass the award within three months of receiving all requirements from the complainant.

> The Ombudsman shall finalise its findings and pass an award within a period of three months of the receipt of all requirements from the complainant.

Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (as amended up to 09.11.2023; CIO consolidated copy), Rule 17(4), page 11 (knowledge-base/regulations/insurance-ombudsman-rules-2017-consolidated-2023.pdf). Effective: 2023-11-09.
Use when: Setting expectations on Ombudsman timelines; follow up if pending beyond 3 months after all documents were submitted.
Challenge strength: moderate

## R-OMBUDSMAN-AWARD-BINDING-ON-INSURER

An Ombudsman award is binding on the insurer.

> The award of Insurance Ombudsman shall be binding on the insurers 1 [or insurance broker, as the case may be].

Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (as amended up to 09.11.2023; CIO consolidated copy), Rule 17(8), page 11 (knowledge-base/regulations/insurance-ombudsman-rules-2017-consolidated-2023.pdf). Effective: 2023-11-09.
Use when: Insurer ignores or delays paying an award.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-OMBUDSMAN-AWARD-30-DAYS-PENALTY-5000

The insurer must comply with an Ombudsman award within 30 days of receipt; otherwise it must pay the complainant Rs 5,000 per day as penalty in addition to penal interest - unless the insurer has chosen to appeal.

> The Insurer is required to comply with the award of the Insurance Ombudsman within 30 days of receipt of award by the Insurer. In case the Insurer does not honour the ombudsman award, a penalty of Rs. 5000/- per day shall be payable to the complainant. Such penalty is in addition to the penal interest liable to be paid by the Insurer under the Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017. This provision will not be applicable in case insurer chooses to appeal against the award of the Insurance Ombudsman.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 2, Chapter VII, para 25.4 (Ombudsman Awards), page 148 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Award in the policyholder's favour not paid within 30 days.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-OMBUDSMAN-AWARD-PENAL-INTEREST

On an Ombudsman award, the insurer must also pay penal interest at bank rate + 2% from the date the claim ought to have been settled.

> The insurer, in terms of rule 17 (7) of Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017, shall also pay penal interest at a rate, which is 2 percent above bank rate from the date the claim ought to have been settled.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 2, Chapter VII, para 25.2 (Ombudsman Awards), page 148 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Calculating amounts due under an Ombudsman award; include interest in the relief sought.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-OMBUDSMAN-MEDIATION-ACCEPTANCE-IS-FINAL

If the Ombudsman mediates and the complainant accepts the recommendation in writing within 15 days, it is accepted as full and final settlement.

> If the recommendation of the Ombudsman is acceptable to the complainant, he shall send a communication in writing within fifteen days of receipt of the recommendation, stating clearly that he accepts the settlement as full and final.

Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017 (as amended up to 09.11.2023; CIO consolidated copy), Rule 16(2) (Complaints settled through mediation), page 10 (knowledge-base/regulations/insurance-ombudsman-rules-2017-consolidated-2023.pdf). Effective: 2023-11-09.
Use when: Policyholder is offered a mediated settlement - warn that acceptance closes the dispute.
Challenge strength: context_dependent

## R-OMBUDSMAN-INSURER-SCN-7-DAYS

Insurers must file their Self-Contained Note with supporting documents within 7 days of the Ombudsman office's notice (and additional documents within 3 days, in one go).

> To submit the required SCNs duly supported by the relevant documents within seven (7) days from the date of receipt of the notice from the concerned Office of the Insurance Ombudsman.

Source: IRDAI Circular: Submission of Self-Contained Note and other related documents to the Office of the Insurance Ombudsmen, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/95/07/2026, Para 3(i), page 2 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-circular-ombudsman-self-contained-note-2026.pdf). Effective: 2026-07-23.
Use when: Ombudsman hearing delayed because the insurer has not filed its reply/SCN.
Challenge strength: moderate

## R-OMBUDSMAN-EX-PARTE-IF-INSURER-DELAYS

If the insurer does not submit the SCN/documents on time, the Ombudsman office will proceed ex parte on the material on record to meet the award timeline.

> Where an insurer fails to comply with the above, the concerned Office of the Insurance Ombudsman, in order to comply with the timeline provided in Rule 17 (4), shall proceed with the matter, ex parte, without any further delay, basis the material information available on record.

Source: IRDAI Circular: Submission of Self-Contained Note and other related documents to the Office of the Insurance Ombudsmen, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/95/07/2026, Para 3(v), page 2 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-circular-ombudsman-self-contained-note-2026.pdf). Effective: 2026-07-23.
Use when: Insurer stalls Ombudsman proceedings; complainant can request the office to proceed ex parte.
Challenge strength: moderate
