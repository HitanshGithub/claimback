# IRDAI / regulatory rules: waiting period

## R-SPECIFIC-WAITING-MAX-36M

A specific-disease/treatment waiting period can be at most 36 months from policy commencement, does not apply to treatment needed due to an accident, and the disease/treatment must be covered once the period is completed with continuous renewal.

> "Specific waiting period" means a period up to 36 months from the commencement of a health insurance policy during which period specified diseases/treatments (except due to an accident) are not covered. On completion of the period, diseases/treatments shall be covered provided the policy has been continuously renewed without any break.

Source: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024, Schedule III, clause 1.7 (definition of Specific waiting period), page 36 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-insurance-products-regulations-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-04-01.
Use when: Rejection citing a specific-disease waiting period (e.g. cataract, hernia, joint replacement) after 36 months of continuous cover, or where the treatment was necessitated by an accident.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-GRACE-PERIOD-CREDITS-PROTECTED

If the policy is renewed within the grace period (15 days for monthly premium, 30 days otherwise), all accrued credits - sum insured, no claim bonus, specific and PED waiting periods, moratorium - remain protected.

> If the policy is renewed during grace period, all the credits (sum insured, No Claim Bonus, Specific Waiting periods, waiting periods for pre-existing diseases, Moratorium period etc.) accrued under the policy shall be protected. The same is applicable for both Indemnity and Benefit products.

Source: Master Circular on IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations 2024 - Health Insurance (Master Circular on Health Insurance Business), Ref IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024, Chapter I, para 8(b) (Grace Period for payment of premium), page 7 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-health-insurance-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-05-29.
Use when: Insurer treats a renewal paid within the grace period as a 'break in policy' and restarts waiting periods or the moratorium count.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-MIGRATION-CREDITS

When moving to another policy with the same insurer, accrued credits (sum insured, NCB, specific and PED waiting periods, moratorium) carry over to the migrated policy.

> In case of migration of one policy to another with the same Insurer, the policyholder (including all members under family cover and group insurance policies) can transfer the credits gained to the extent of the Sum Insured, No Claim Bonus, Specific Waiting periods, waiting period for pre-existing diseases, Moratorium period etc. in the previous policy to the migrated policy.

Source: Master Circular on IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations 2024 - Health Insurance (Master Circular on Health Insurance Business), Ref IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024, Chapter I, para 11 (Migration in case of Indemnity policies), page 8 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-health-insurance-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-05-29.
Use when: Claim rejected for waiting period/PED/non-disclosure after the policyholder migrated plans within the same insurer and the insurer ignored time served under the earlier policy.
Challenge strength: strong

## R-PORTABILITY-CREDITS

On porting to a new insurer, credits for sum insured, NCB, specific waiting periods, PED waiting period and moratorium transfer from the existing insurer to the acquiring insurer.

> By porting, the policyholder is entitled to transfer the credits gained to the extent of the Sum Insured, No Claim Bonus, specific waiting periods, waiting period for pre-existing disease, Moratorium period etc. from the Existing Insurer to the Acquiring Insurer in the previous policy.

Source: Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024, Ref IRDAI/PP&GR/CIR/MISC/117/9/2024, Section 1, Part B, VII(3)(ii) (Portability in case of Health Indemnity Policies), page 125 (knowledge-base/regulations/irdai-master-circular-protection-of-policyholders-interests-2024.pdf). Effective: 2024-09-05.
Use when: Acquiring insurer rejects a claim for PED/specific waiting period or non-disclosure by counting only time since port-in and ignoring continuous cover with the previous insurer (to the extent of the previous sum insured).
Challenge strength: strong
